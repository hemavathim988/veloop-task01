Hemavathi M · Intern ID VLRINT20263720 · Task 01 · Prototype built on simulated data to test KPI definitions

SIMULATED DATA — CREATED FOR ANALYTICAL TESTING. Not actual VELOOP data or performance. Users, sessions, events and tasks come from the supplied simulated files; reward figures come from analyst-generated stand-in data.

Daily active users and new registrations

DAU = unique users with a qualifying event per day (KPI-U02). Registrations: KPI-U01.

Task completion rate by week

Completed ÷ all attempts (KPI-E06); respects the category filter.

Task outcomes by category

Completed, abandoned, failed, pending attempts (KPI-E05–E07).

Why tasks fail or are abandoned

Failure-reason mix (simulated; reasons are near-uniform by construction).

Reward distribution by unit and status

Credited amount per unit (never summed across units; KPI-R02). Stand-in data, not filtered by category.

Feature usage

Events per feature after cleaning (KPI-E02 input).
Definitions. Completion rate = Completed ÷ all attempts. Abandonment = Abandoned ÷ attempts. Failure = Failed ÷ attempts. Pending share = Pending ÷ attempts. Reversal rate = Reversed ÷ (Credited + Reversed + Corrected). Average DAU = mean of daily unique active users in the range. Limitations. Differences between categories in this simulated data are small and should not be read as business insight; the prototype exists to prove the KPI formulas and the data model work. Rewards are not linked to task category in the filter. Replace with governed production data when available.
